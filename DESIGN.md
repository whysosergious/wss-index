# Design System: Sergio Stankevich Portfolio

A modern, high-fidelity portfolio design system focused on professional software engineering, automation, and full-stack expertise. The aesthetic is clean and premium, balancing deep navy tones with vibrant accents and smooth interactive motion.

## Brand Identity

**Persona:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer / Automation Expert
**Tone:** Professional, reliable, efficient, and technically sophisticated.
**Keywords:** Clean, high-performance, automated, pixel-perfect.

---

## Color Palette

### Primary & Accent

- **Primary Purple:** `#8b5cf6` (used for the "Stankevich" highlight)
- **Primary White:** `#ffffff` (used for primary text and brand name)
- **Secondary Accent:** Green `#22c55e` (used for the "Available" status and success indicators)
- **Subtle Gray:** `#94a3b8` (used for secondary text and labels)

### Backgrounds & Surfaces

- **Global Background:** `#ffffff` (sections) / `#0f172a` (hero section)
- **Surface Deep:** `#110e25` (footer and sticky header)
- **Surface Light:** `#f8fafc` (card backgrounds and alternate sections)
- **Surface Hover:** `#f1f5f9` (interactive elements)

---

## Typography

**Typeface:** Sans-serif (Inter or similar modern grotesque)

### Hierarchy

- **Heading 1 (Hero):** 4rem / 64px, bold, tight letter-spacing.
- **Heading 2 (Section):** 2.5rem / 40px, bold.
- **Heading 3 (Card Titles):** 1.25rem / 20px, bold.
- **Body:** 1rem / 16px, line-height 1.6, medium weight.
- **Labels/Tags:** 0.75rem / 12px, uppercase, bold.

---

## Components

### Navigation

- **Sticky Header:** Deep navy background (`#110e25`) with white text.
- **Desktop Links:** Clean, minimalist text links on the right.
- **Mobile Menu:** Burger icon toggle on the right; "Sergio" logo on the left.

### Buttons

- **Primary (CTA):** Pill-shaped, white background, black text. Subtle scale animation on hover.
- **Secondary (Outline):** Pill-shaped, transparent background with white border.
- **Hover States:** Magnetic lift effects and subtle shadow deepening.

### Cards

- **Project Cards:**
  - White background with soft border (`#e2e8f0`).
  - Image container at the top.
  - Category tags (e.g., "Typescript", "Next.js") in small, subtle blocks.
  - Hover: Smooth elevation and shadow transition.
- **Before/After Cards:**
  - Contrasting vertical layout showing problem vs. automated solution.
  - Arrow transition icon in the center.

---

## Motion & Interactions

- **Reveal Animations:** Scroll-triggered fade-ins and slides for all major sections.
- **Micro-interactions:**
  - Magnetic buttons.
  - Elevated project cards on hover.
  - Pulse/shimmer effect on tech stack icons.
- **Transitions:** Smooth CSS transforms (0.3s ease-in-out) for all interactive states.

---

## Icons

- **Tech Stack:** Circular containers with brand-specific colors (e.g., TS-blue, Vue-green, React-cyan).
- **Service Icons:** Minimalist line-art icons for "Custom Curtain Orders", "Shipping Labels", etc.
