From Manual Work to Shipped

The point of the work isn't the code — it's what changes for the business once it's running. A couple of real examples.

Custom Curtain Orders, End to End

Before

Two employees handled every custom order by hand — reading dimensions, sketching the shape, calculating the price, and emailing a quote. Slow, and mistakes led to returns.

After

Customers configure the exact product on the site. The order runs itself: PDF drawing to the plotter, PostNord shipping, invoice, and account update — no one touches it manually.

See how

Shipping Labels for 20–50 Parcels a Day

Before

Every shipment took 5–7 minutes of manual work: validating the address, booking PostNord, printing the label, and updating records by hand.

After

One API call does all of it — address validation, PostNord booking, thermal label, and tracking. Manual work gone, and shipment errors down to a few isolated cases a month.

Are We a Good Fit?

You're Probably Looking for Someone Like Me If...

  • Your team has a heavy manual workload software should be doing
  • You've outgrown spreadsheet tools and need real systems behind the process
  • Your developers are stretched thin and things are starting to slip
  • Integrations keep breaking and nobody actually owns them
  • Stripe (or any payment flow) doesn't map cleanly onto how your business works
  • You want one person who understands the code and the business behind it

I'm Probably Not the Right Fit If...

  • You're hunting for the cheapest freelancer you can find
  • You already have a large in-house engineering team
  • You just need pixel-perfect HTML/CSS from a figma design
  • You're looking for a designer, not a technical partner
